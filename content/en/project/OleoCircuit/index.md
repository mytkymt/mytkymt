OleoCircuit explores **edible conductive gels that can be integrated into food**. It combines oil-based oleogels and water-based hydrogels with edible conductive fillers.

Changing the oil-to-water ratio adjusts texture and fabrication properties. The study demonstrates extrusion-based food printing and conductive traces integrated with chocolate. It investigates materials and fabrication methods for incorporating sensing elements into food.

The work was presented as a poster at ACM UIST 2025.
